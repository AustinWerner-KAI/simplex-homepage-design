Your connections.
Your control.

Most messengers know who you are and who you talk to. SimpleX is built so that no server can.

No user identity
There is no account, number or username that identifies you across the network.
No central server
Messages pass through independent relays. You can run your own. No single point of control.
Your data stays with you
Contacts, groups and history live on your device, encrypted end to end at every hop.
